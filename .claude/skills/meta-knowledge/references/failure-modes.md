# meta-knowledge — failure modes and what the prose may assume

Open this when foreknowledge is not landing — it is always one of seven named shapes — or when deciding how much of the source the prose may assume a reader knows.

---

## 6. What the prose may assume of the reader

Declared once, in `bible/canon.md` (fanfic) or the bible's world file:

| stance | the prose | risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Rewards** knowledge | reads completely to a newcomer; a reader who knows the source gets an extra layer | none — this is the default and the target |
| **Requires** knowledge | a newcomer is lost | halves the audience and reads as lazy |
| **Explains** everything | every reference annotated | bores the audience you actually have |

**The MC's foreknowledge is not a licence to skip exposition.** It is the opposite: because he
knows what a name means, the *narration* can move fast, but the reader still needs the anchor
(`story-opening` §1). A name the MC recognises with dread is an anchor and an explanation at once —
his reaction *is* the exposition.

## 7. Failure modes

| symptom | cause | fix |
|---|---|---|
| Foreknowledge never appears on the page | it lives in `novel.md` and nowhere else | the inventory scene (§2), by `first_win_by_ch` |
| First foreknowledge beat is it being unreliable | decay scheduled before use | §4 — reorder. A win first |
| The MC knows something at the wrong precision | grain not held | §1 — an `impressions` MC cannot name a date |
| The MC never plans | author avoiding a plot that outruns them | §2 — good plan, incomplete information |
| Everything still happens on the source's schedule | no observer paradox | §5, and `timeline-engine`'s impact rule |
| The MC quotes things they never witnessed | wiki-voice | `competence-map` provenance test |
| Foreknowledge substitutes for competence | axes conflated | §3 — knowing the answer is not knowing the method |
| The MC is dumb so foreknowledge stays useful | idiot ball | `mc-intel-meter`. Never |
| Nobody ever notices the MC knows too much | free advantage | `gf_problem`, and §6 of the ledger |
