# timeline-engine — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` **Step 1**, every chapter. Written here rather than summarised there,
because the offstage question is one line and is the reason a reader believes the world is running
at all — and it is the first thing dropped when a chapter is drafted from the MC outward.

**Produce:** the offstage question — *what did the world do this chapter that the MC does not know
about?* One line, for the CCS `wld>` line.

Most chapters it is small: a price moves, a rival's letter arrives somewhere else, a canon event
stays pending. It is not small only because nothing happened; it is small because the MC was not
there.

## Pitch it to the dial

`timeline.reactivity` is in the read-set CONFIG.

| dial | the world |
|---|---|
| 1 **Inert** | events fire on baseline chapters; the MC affects only their scene |
| 2 **Ripples** | local and personal changes stick; major beats still land roughly on time |
| 3 **Responsive** | opposition notices, adapts, re-plans. Some beats move, one or two are prevented. **Default** |
| 4 **Adaptive** | the opposition models the MC specifically and allocates against them |
| 5 **Predatory** | the world reorganises around the MC — to recruit, use or remove |

Dials 4–5 are **not** nightmare mode: intensity of reaction and cruelty of outcome are separate axes.

## If the MC touches the world track this chapter

It gets a divergence row — `moved` · `prevented` · `altered` · `created` · `unchanged` (recorded
deliberately, with a reason). Simulate first- and second-order effects on the page; third-order as
**background** — a rumour, a changed price, a canon event that now fires three chapters late;
fourth-order and beyond is narrated at an arc boundary, never simulated.

**The point-of-no-return check, before firing any world-track event:** does this make
`ending.contract` unreachable? If yes, do not fire it as written — change its scale, timing or
target. The world may cost the MC enormously; it may never close the road. Anything on
`ending.non_negotiables` is protected absolutely.

**Every arc moves at least one world-track event** — `unchanged` does not count. For fanfic, by the
end of arc 1 a canon character has *changed a decision* because of the MC. Not been impressed;
changed a decision.

A faction is about to react, or escalation is running hot: `references/reaction-and-governor.md`.
Fanfic, where this skill does most of its work: `references/fanfic-mode.md`.
