# no-harem — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 6**, with bias, when the module is on — which is the default.
This is a distributional check: its defects are invisible in one chapter and obvious across ten,
so read the chapter against the cast file, not on its own.

## The gate

> **Is there a character in this novel who is indifferent to the MC?**

Universal attraction is not romance; it is wish-fulfilment scaffolding, and it is the shape the
corpus reaches for unprompted. If nobody is indifferent, the defect is already present whatever
this chapter did.

## Checks

- [ ] One active romantic line, or a named reason there is more than one
- [ ] Each party's attraction is specific and stated — not that she is beautiful or he is strong
- [ ] Every love interest pursued an on-page goal unrelated to the MC this arc
- [ ] No rejection has softened. A decision to decline holds, and does not erode over chapters
- [ ] Nobody was awarded to anyone for winning, for power, or for kindness
- [ ] Any romantic rival wants the same person for their own reasons, and is not thereby a
      villain, an idiot, or someone the plot will remove
- [ ] Nobody is orbiting: no character appears mainly to demonstrate continued devotion
- [ ] Not every named woman is romantically available while no named man is

## The draft tells

These are what the defect looks like at chapter scale, before the pattern is visible:

- A character introduced with an appearance inventory and a blush in the same chapter
- Two or more characters whose scenes exist chiefly to register feelings about the MC
- A jealousy scene that resolves without either party changing
- Anyone in `bible/cast/` whose stated want is only about the MC

If `optional.no-harem` is `off`, rules 2 and 5 through 9 above still hold — they are `bias-guard`
requirements in romantic clothing, and that skill is not toggleable.
