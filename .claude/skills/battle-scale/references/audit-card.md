# battle-scale — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 4** when the module is on and the chapter contains an
engagement. Skip in one line otherwise.

## The gate

> **Did anyone know something they had no channel for?**

This is the failure that makes battle prose read as a board game. A character who knows the left
has broken, before a runner, a horn, or a change in the noise told them, is being narrated from
above. The repair is cheap and always improves the scene: give them the wrong version first, from
somebody out of breath.

## Checks

- [ ] One frame — body, decision, or line — held for the whole engagement
- [ ] A reader could state the POV character's objective, what losing reaches, and how they would
      find out
- [ ] No establishing paragraph of dispositions, and no troop numbers doing work that sound,
      time, or what people are eating should do
- [ ] The outcome arrived late, partial, and from somebody who might be wrong
- [ ] Attrition is statistical **and** specific: a fraction, and one name the reader knew
- [ ] Units degraded and broke rather than being killed to the last
- [ ] The engagement turned on cohesion, not on who killed more
- [ ] Any individual heroics worked through other people's behaviour
- [ ] The aftermath is on the page — the counting, the burying, the wounded, the garrison
- [ ] Something outside the battle is different, and it is not only who is now stronger

## Where it fails most often

| symptom | what it actually is | go to |
|---|---|---|
| The chapter reads as a report | the frame slipped from body to decision mid-scene | `SKILL.md` §1 — pick one and go back |
| Thousands died and the chapter feels weightless | attrition without a name in it | `SKILL.md` §4 |
| The MC personally decided the field | a scaling problem wearing armour | `power-scaling` — make the field bigger |
| Everyone fought to the death | battles are decided by routs, and most dying happens afterwards | `SKILL.md` §4 |
| The battle is won and nothing else moved | the engagement cost nothing | `conflict-engine` |
| The army arrived fed, dry and on time | the campaign was skipped | `references/logistics.md` |
