# power-scaling — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` **Step 1**, every chapter, unless `scaling.shape` is `none`. Written here
rather than summarised there, because the order of two decisions is the whole skill, and getting it
backwards is what produces a cast of cannon fodder.

**Produce:** the pressure this chapter wants, and only then who the opposition is.

## Pressure, not magnitude

> **`P = opposition tier − MC tier`**

| P | reads as | the rule |
|---|---|---|
| **+2** or worse | hopeless | the MC cannot win by force. They win by not-force, or they lose. Opens arcs, rarely closes them |
| **+1** | outmatched | the workhorse. A win here costs something that stays paid |
| **0** | even | the win comes from preparation, information or choice — the most interesting band |
| **−1** | favoured | the fight is fast, so name where the chapter's interest actually is |
| **−2** or better | trivial | not a scene unless its point is something other than the contest. Budgeted by `scaling.trivial_per_arc` |

**Two steps, in this order.** (1) What pressure does this chapter want — from the arc's band in
`state/power.md` §6? (2) What tier does that make the opposition? Then build a person who is that
tier. **Never the reverse.**

The MC gets stronger *and the fights stay hard*: that is the entire trick, and losing it is the flat
middle. Reader interest tracks the gap, never the MC's absolute tier.

## The edge is not a tier

`mc.golden_finger` buys `scaling.edge_worth`, which is **0 or 1, never 2** — it closes a gap of at
most one tier, at `scaling.edge_price`, paid every time. Using it well does **not** move the MC up
the ladder; only a gain row does. `edge_worth: 0` is a real answer.

## If capability changes on the page

A permanent advance is **at most +1 and never arrives alone**. Four requirements, all on the page:
a **source** with its own interests (nothing found in a cave by luck) · a **price paid before** the
gain lands (paid after, it is a receipt; paid before, it is a decision) · a **setup**
`scaling.setup_lead` chapters earlier · a **new problem**. Minimum `scaling.gain_gap_min` chapters
between advances; between them, growth is in *skill*, which reads as intelligence and costs nothing.

A **boost** is temporary reach with an expiry and a **debt** decided before use. Its honest job is
to let the MC *survive* a P≥+2 encounter, not win it. It may resolve an arc climax **at most once in
the novel**.

Tier names never appear in the prose. Choosing or changing the shape: `references/curve-shapes.md`.
The curve is not landing: `references/failure-modes.md`.
