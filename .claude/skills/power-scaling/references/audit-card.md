# power-scaling — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 9e**, whenever `scaling.shape` is not `none`. It runs after the
delivery gate because a chapter that delivers nothing has no curve to audit, and before the
mechanical wrap-up so a corrected tier still reaches the frontmatter and the CCS block. `sw curve`
finds the countable half — step size, cadence, unpaid debts, monotony, the trivial budget, a
pressure value that disagrees with the ledger. It cannot tell whether a gain was *earned on the
page*, whether an opponent is a person or a number, or whether a win felt free, so those stay here.

- [ ] Every confrontation has a pressure value in `state/power.md` §3, and the CCS `pwr>` line
      agrees with it
- [ ] The pressure was decided **before** the opponent — no opponent invented at the strength the
      scene needed and priced afterwards
- [ ] The chapter sits inside its arc's planned band (§6), or the deviation is deliberate and the
      plan row says so
- [ ] Nothing described the MC as more or less capable than `state/power.md` §1 says they are
- [ ] The edge closed **at most one tier**, paid `scaling.edge_price` in the same scene, and did
      not quietly do the work of a tier advance
- [ ] Anything the edge cannot buy is still something it cannot buy
- [ ] **Any tier advance is +1** and carries all four: a source with its own interests, a price
      paid before the gain landed, a setup at least `scaling.setup_lead` chapters back, and a new
      problem. A gain missing one of these is a defect, not a style choice
- [ ] Any boost states how far above tier it reached, when it expires, and what it will cost —
      and **did not resolve a second arc climax**
- [ ] A win at P ≤ −1 was not the point of the scene; name what was
- [ ] A win at P ≥ +1 cost something that is still costing at the end of the chapter
- [ ] No capability decided the outcome that the reader has not seen used *and seen fail*
- [ ] No tier name, rank recital or ladder recap in the prose (`mtl-detox`)
- [ ] Opening arc only: the advantage has landed its first win by `opening.first_win_by_ch`, and
      the first limit is still ahead of it or landed on schedule

If a box fails, open `SKILL.md`: §1 for pressure and placing an opponent, §2 for the edge, §3 for
the step rule and boosts, §4 for the curve. If the curve itself is the problem rather than this
chapter, open `references/failure-modes.md`.

## What it looks like when it lands

She wins by knowing the building, not by being stronger: P = +1, and the win costs her the hand
she needs for next week's work. No tier moved. The gap narrowed because the opposition made a
mistake, which is where most of a curve's movement should come from.
