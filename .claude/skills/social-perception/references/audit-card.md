# social-perception — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 3b**, straight after the knowledge checks — a claim about what
somebody is feeling is a knowledge claim, and it needs a provenance the same way a fact does.

## The gate

> **Did anyone state another character's interior state as a fact?**

*She had decided to refuse him.* *He was frightened of his father.* In a limited viewpoint those
are not observations, they are somebody else's head, and the prose has changed POV without saying
so. The repair is one clause: give the reader what this character can actually see, and let them
conclude it themselves. That is almost always the stronger line anyway.

## Checks

- [ ] Every conclusion about a person sits inside that character's `eq` tier — nobody read intent
      at tier 2, nobody missed open weeping at tier 4
- [ ] The signal each reading rests on is **on the page**, in this chapter
- [ ] Nobody's read of another person was simply correct, every time, without effort
- [ ] Emotions reached the page as this character's **signature**, not as the shared gesture set
      (`voice-separation` owns the ban; the signatures are in the profiles)
- [ ] No emotion was named outright where behaviour would carry it (`prose-quality`)
- [ ] Any misread satisfies all four conditions — signal present, reading reasonable, true cause
      visible, and it cost something
- [ ] Nobody was made socially blind to let the MC notice something. The cast straddles the MC's
      `eq` the way it straddles their intel
- [ ] A character with higher `eq` than the POV did something with what they read

## Where it fails most often

| symptom | what it actually is | go to |
|---|---|---|
| The POV character always knows what everyone means | `eq` was never set, so it defaults to the author's | `SKILL.md` §1 — pick a tier and hold it |
| Everyone reads everyone perfectly and the plot runs on secrets | the secrets are surviving only because nobody looks | `SKILL.md` §2 — the gap rule |
| A misread reads as the character being dim | condition 2 failed: the reading was not the one their tier produces | make it the reasonable reading, or use a different lever |
| Emotion arrives as a clenched jaw | the default gesture set filling the vacuum | the four signature entries in their profile |
| A tier-5 reader also has their own life in order | the error profile was skipped | `SKILL.md` §1 — it is not optional |
