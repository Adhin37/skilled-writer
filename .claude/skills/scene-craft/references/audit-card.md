# scene-craft — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 4** (structure) and **Pass 9** (delivery). The draft card asks
these questions of a chapter that does not exist yet; this one asks them of a draft, where the
honest answer is often different from the planned one.

`scene-craft` owns the **delivery test**. The answers are written into the chapter's `delivers:`
field, and Pass Z's answer into `event:` — two fields that fail in opposite directions.

## The delivery test

One sentence each, and none of them strained.

| | question | fails when |
|---|---|---|
| **Want** | What did the POV character want here? | The honest answer is "to react to things." A chapter with no want is a chapter of weather |
| **Friction** | What person, rule or scarcity stood in the way? | The only obstacle is the MC's own hesitation, twice running |
| **Change** | **What is materially different at the end?** | The exit state equals the entry state. This is the load-bearing question |
| **Cost** | What was paid, by whom? | Nothing — `conflict-engine` owns the repair |
| **Next** | What does the reader now need to see? | The hook asks a question the chapter already answered |

**Change is not a summary of events.** *"He asks about the recount"* is events; *"he is now
someone Tsuru watches on purpose"* is a change. A model asked only for "a difference" will name the
smallest one it can find and write eleven hundred words around it, which is why Pass Z gates on the
event first.

## The scenes inside it

- [ ] Every scene has a goal, an obstacle with its own reason, a turn, and a cost
- [ ] No scene exits on a plain yes — `Yes, but` · `No, and` · `No, but` · `Yes, and` (rare)
- [ ] The chapter's cost is stateable in one concrete sentence, and is not a feeling
- [ ] The chapter entered late and left early: deleting the first and last paragraph makes it worse
- [ ] A repeated beat escalated — a second refusal costs more than the first, or one of them is cut

## Where it fails most often

| symptom | what it actually is | go to |
|---|---|---|
| Want and friction answer, change does not | the chapter is a scene, not a chapter | `story-craft` — a beat was reported that needed playing |
| Every answer lands but the chapter reads thin | the event got a summary and a bridge got the scene | `story-craft` §the weight test |
| Change is an interior shift in somebody's feelings | the delivery gate is being satisfied in the abstract | Pass Z. Name what *happened*, with a verb and a target |
| Cost is paid by someone offstage | nothing was risked by the POV character | `conflict-engine` |
