# power-system — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` Phase A whenever the genre module is live. Decides what power does in
this chapter, not how the system is designed — for design, open `SKILL.md`.

**Produce three lines for the brief.** If nothing in the chapter uses a capability, write
`power: none` and close this card.

```
uses     Echo-step, to reach the gate before the patrol rounds the corner
pays     six hours of recovery she does not have - she is slow for the rest of the chapter
new      the sound it makes; the patrol captain hears it and now knows someone was there
```

## The three decisions

1. **Which capability, and has the reader seen it?** A capability may only resolve a problem the
   reader has watched it handle *and* watched it fail at. If the answer is "they have not", the
   chapter can still use it to create a problem, threaten, or mystify — it may not settle
   anything.
2. **What it takes, paid in this scene.** Name the unit from `bible/power-system.md`. Not
   narrated afterwards, not deferred to the next chapter. If the cost cannot be shown here, the
   use is in the wrong chapter.
3. **What it leaves behind.** Visible use is evidence; somebody can have noticed. Fuel runs out
   and somebody sells it. Name the residue, or accept that the chapter's power beat is free —
   which `conflict-engine` owns and forbids.

## While drafting

- **Second use is shorter than first use.** A capability the reader knows does not get its
  introduction again; spend the words on what is new about today.
- **Sensory signature, reused.** The same two details every time — what it does to the air, the
  body, the light, the sound. That is how a reader recognises it mid-fight without being told.
- **No mechanics mid-action.** If the reader needs the rule now, they needed it two chapters ago.
- **No rank recital**, and the MC's own position stays partly unclear to the MC (`mtl-detox`).
- A permanent gain is not this card's decision. `power-scaling` owns whether one lands, at what
  price, and its row in `state/power.md` §4.

If a decision will not settle, open `power-system/SKILL.md` §Writing power on the page, or
§The four hard rules when the cost or limit is what is missing.
