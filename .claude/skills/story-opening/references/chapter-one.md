# story-opening — chapter 1's shape, and integration

Open this when actually drafting chapter 1, or when wiring the opening arc into the planning and revision loops.

---

## 5. Chapter 1's shape

Proportions, not word counts. A chapter is judged by what it delivers
(`revision-pass` Pass 9), and these are the four things chapter 1 delivers.

| share | phase | job | never |
|---|---|---|---|
| first ~20% | **Disruption** | Something is already wrong. A curiosity gap opens early | Waking from a dream · weather · "My name is…" · a prologue in disguise |
| ~25% | **Investment** | A specific person with a concrete present want and a visible flaw | Tragedy as a substitute for specificity · backstory |
| ~35% | **World** | The anchor (§1), delivered as consequence | "In this world…" · a rank ladder · a magic-system lecture |
| final ~20% | **Cliffhanger** | A decision, a revelation or an emotional turn that makes ch 2 unavoidable | A question the chapter already answered |

**The protagonist must act.** A first chapter in which the MC only observes, reflects, or is
delivered somewhere by other people fails regardless of prose quality — readers read narrative
momentum as authorial competence.

**Openings to avoid** are in `hook-and-pacing` §Openings; that list still governs. This skill adds
one: **do not open on a date-and-place stamp** to solve the anchor problem. `hook-and-pacing`
§Openings bans it and is right. The sanctioned alternatives are §1's channels 1–3 — a rule biting
someone, a character working around the world, an assumed reference.

## 6. Integration

| when | do |
|---|---|
| `novel-init` | Fill the whole `opening:` block. `promise` is written from the blurb, in the user's words |
| `chapter-plan` | Rows 1–5 carry the anchor, contract, promise and first-win beats explicitly. No escalation rung is scheduled before the frame it depends on (§3) |
| `write-chapter` step 1 | While chapter ≤ `contract_by_ch + 2`: what does this chapter owe the ledger? |
| `revision-pass` | The opening pass, on every chapter in scope. **Open this file; do not work from the checklist summary** — §1 and §3 are judgement calls a checklist cannot make |
| Arc 1 close | The ledger is fully paid, or the arc does not close |
