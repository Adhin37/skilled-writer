# mystery-clues — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 3**, beside the knowledge checks, when the module is on. Fair
play is a knowledge property, which is why it audits here rather than with structure.

## The gate

> **Did the narration state anything false?**

The POV character may be wrong about anything. The prose may not assert something untrue. Omission
and misinterpretation are fair; a false statement is a broken contract, and readers who catch one
stop trusting every sentence afterwards. This is the only unrecoverable failure in the form.

## Checks

- [ ] Every clue this chapter touched is in the ledger, with both knowledge columns updated
- [ ] The chapter's knowledge mode matches the plan row, and is not the third one in a row
- [ ] No `ahead` chapter asked the POV character to miss what they witnessed
- [ ] Any clue planted is doing a second job on the page, so it reads as something else
- [ ] Any red herring introduced has an innocent explanation that the story will give
- [ ] The decisive clue has been planted at least twice before any reveal that uses it
- [ ] A reveal, if one landed, cost someone something and forced an unwelcome decision
- [ ] No reveal explained its own foreshadowing back to the reader

## Where it fails most often

| symptom | what it actually is | go to |
|---|---|---|
| The reveal is a character explaining at length | the drawing-room recitation | `story-craft` — dramatise it, under pressure, to someone who will act |
| The clue was fair but nobody saw it | disguised by obscurity rather than by function | `SKILL.md` §Planting |
| The misdirection is never accounted for | a hole wearing a red herring's coat | `plot-threads` — it is an open promise |
| The answer changed nothing | trivia | `conflict-engine` — knowing has to cost |
