# competence-map — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 3**, every chapter. Covers **every character in the chapter**,
not only the MC.

`sw cast` has already checked the budget arithmetic and the missing rows. Provenance is what is
left, and it is the whole job.

- [ ] **Provenance test** on every stated fact: they were taught it, did it, were told it, read
      it, or are openly guessing. *"They're intelligent"* is not a provenance
- [ ] Nobody answered inside a domain their row in `bible/cast/_competence.md` does not list.
      **An unlisted domain is `none`**, not "probably fine"
- [ ] Someone in this chapter said they did not know, went to their referral, or was confidently
      wrong. If nobody did, the cast is reading as omniscient
- [ ] No exposition handed to a convenient mouth: nobody explained a subject outside their map
- [ ] Nobody improvised a second specialty where a referral existed
- [ ] Any skill practised advanced by a teacher, a reference, or a costly failure — never by
      elapsed time. A stage transition got a scene; ordinary practice got a clause
- [ ] Practice cost something namable this chapter
- [ ] Broad-knowledge characters (`knowledge_scope: broad`) stayed inside their declared shape,
      hit their declared boundary, and had their **access** bounded rather than their knowledge

**When a gap is found**, prefer the cheap fix, in this order:

1. Give the line to someone whose map covers it.
2. Have the character ask their referral.
3. Let them be *wrong* about it — this usually improves the scene.
4. Only last, add the domain to their map. That is a permanent change to who they are.

If a box fails, open `SKILL.md`: §1 for the map and the four rules, §3 for provenance, §5 for the
acquisition ladder, §6 for the broad-knowledge clause.
