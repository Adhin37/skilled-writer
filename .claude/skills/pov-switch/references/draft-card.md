# pov-switch — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` **Step 1**, every chapter. Written here rather than summarised there,
because the answer is usually "no switch" and that answer should cost one glance, not a skill load.

**Produce:** the POV character, and whether this chapter is a switch.

## Settle it from the read-set CONFIG first

`pov.mode: single` + `switch_granularity: never` → **the answer is no. Stop here.** Nobody outside
`pov.pov_characters` narrates, and adding a name to that list is an arc-boundary decision.

Mid-scene switching is forbidden in every mode. Two heads in one scene is head-hopping.

## If a switch is on the table, it must pass one

1. **Information the MC cannot have**, and the reader needs it now for dread or irony.
2. **A decision made elsewhere** that will hit the MC later and matters more if seen being made.
3. **A parallel line of action** on its own clock.
4. **An arc that requires being inside their head** — usually a rung advance that would look like
   betrayal from outside.

It fails if it exists to show the MC being impressive from outside, to deliver exposition more
conveniently, to dodge a hard scene in the MC's head, or because the MC is asleep.

## Executing one

- Establish the new head **in the first two sentences**, through idiom, not announcement.
- Their **thought unit** — images, words, numbers, bodies, rules, people, money — supplies this
  chapter's metaphors, and no two POV characters share one. Their **thought speed** sets whether
  the narration arrives at conclusions before or after the reader. A POV whose unit and speed match
  the MC's has changed the name above the camera and nothing else.
- **Hold to their knowledge.** They know what their CCS `kno>` entries say and nothing more. This
  is the highest-risk moment for a continuity break.
- Never re-narrate a shown scene from the other side; once per novel at most.

**Return discipline: never leave the MC for more than two consecutive chapters.**

Choosing the mode at `novel-init`, or the `antagonist_pov` dial: `pov-switch` §Choosing the mode.
