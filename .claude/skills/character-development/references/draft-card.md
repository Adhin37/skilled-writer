# character-development — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` **Step 1**, every chapter. Written here rather than summarised there,
because the question is per-chapter and cheap — *who is different today, and how would a reader
hear it?* — and it is the step that silently gets skipped.

**Produce:** which character sounds different today, and the delta that makes it audible.

## Read `state/growth.md` rows for this chapter's tier-A and B characters

| tier | what they get |
|---|---|
| **A** principal | the full five-rung ladder |
| **B** supporting | **one shift** per arc — two positions, with a voice delta |
| **C** walk-on | nothing. The same person in both scenes they appear in, and that is correct |

A tier-C character who "grows" is a tier-B you have not promoted yet. Take the promotion or cut the
change.

## A rung moves only on a trigger

**Cost paid** (acted on the belief, and it took something unrecoverable) · **Contradiction
witnessed** (someone they respect proves the belief false) · **Failure of the old method** (the thing
that always worked stops) · **Being seen** (someone names the Lie out loud, accurately, at the wrong
moment).

Anything else is a mood. `development_rate` is a budget, not a schedule: a rung advances when its
trigger fires, not when a counter expires. If a trigger fires here, write it **as a scene beat** —
the character does something their earlier self could not, and someone notices.

## The voice delta is the part that matters

A rung advance that changes nothing about how a character *sounds* has not happened. Deltas are
small, mechanical and cumulative — sentence length, hedges dropped, forms of address, asking for
information instead of permission, the word they never said, what they notice, "I" becoming "we".

**Rule: a reader who skipped 50 chapters should be able to tell this line is from later.**

**But deltas must not converge the cast.** Every channel above pushes the same direction — shorter,
surer, more direct — which is the direction of the MC. If the obvious delta would raise a character's
a character through `voice-separation`'s ceiling, choose another: growth also runs toward *more
oblique*, *slower*, *quieter*, *funnier in a worse way* (`voice-separation` §6).

Anyone practising a skill: a stage advances only on a teacher, a text or a costly failure — never on
elapsed time — and practice between stages is a clause, not a scene.
