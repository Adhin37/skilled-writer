# character-development — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 2**. `voice-separation`'s card asks whether the cast still sound
like different people; this one asks whether any of them has **become** a different person, and
whether the reader could hear it.

## The gate

> **Was any growth narrated?**

A sentence telling the reader that somebody has changed, matured, learned or finally understood is
the defect. Growth reaches the page as a choice made differently from the one they made before, or
as a line they could not have said in chapter 4. If the change exists only in narration, delete
the narration and find the choice.

## Checks

- [ ] `state/growth.md` is current for everyone who appeared
- [ ] Every advance was caused by one of the four triggers — not by chapters having passed
- [ ] Every advance has a recorded **voice delta**, and the delta is audible in this chapter's
      dialogue rather than only in the ledger
- [ ] No delta moved anybody onto the MC's axes. A character who grows toward the protagonist's
      register has not developed, they have been absorbed (`voice-separation` owns the ceiling)
- [ ] Any skill stage that advanced is legal under `competence-map`, and changed how they talk
      about the work
- [ ] The MC's pain ledger has an entry for this chapter
- [ ] No tier-A character has sat at rung 1 for two full arcs while on the page
- [ ] No tier-C walk-on was given a rung, an epiphany, or an interior life

## Where it fails most often

| symptom | what it actually is | go to |
|---|---|---|
| The ladder moved but nothing sounds different | the delta was recorded and never spent | `references/draft-card.md` — the delta is a line, not a note |
| Everyone is growing every chapter | development rate is uncalibrated | `SKILL.md` §Rate — most of the cast move slowly |
| A regression happened and then quietly un-happened | a rung was borrowed for one scene | log it or cut it; regressions cost the same as advances |
| The growth is the chapter's stated lesson | the theme is being narrated through a character | `revision-pass` Pass 9d |
