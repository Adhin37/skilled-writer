# conflict-engine — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 4**. `scene-craft`'s card asks whether each scene has a cost;
this one asks whether the *chapter* took something, and from whom.

## The gate

> **State this chapter's cost in one concrete sentence.**

Not a feeling, not a worry, not a lesson. Something a reader could point at: time, money, a
relationship, a position, a capability, an option that is now closed. If the sentence needs the
word *realises*, nothing was paid.

## Checks

- [ ] **At least two of the four conflict sources are active.** A whole chapter of one person
      against one obstacle reads as thin whatever happens in it — and the second source is
      usually already present and unused: a deadline, a bystander with their own stake
- [ ] The antagonist did something this chapter **for their own reasons**, not in response to the
      MC
- [ ] Any victory was set up earlier and priced — nothing was won that was not bought
- [ ] Any failure came from a choice, and its consequences persist past this chapter
- [ ] The cost landed on the POV character, not on somebody offstage
- [ ] At least two threads remain open

## Escalation

The stake ladder moves by what is **lost**, not by who is fought. A chapter whose only escalation
is a stronger opponent has not escalated; it has rescaled. Check against the arc's rung: the world
may climb `timeline.escalation_ceiling` rungs per arc and no more, and a rung that has never been
occupied cannot be skipped to.

## Where it fails most often

| symptom | what it actually is | go to |
|---|---|---|
| The cost is that somebody feels worse | an emotional aftertaste standing in for a price | name what is materially gone |
| Everything was paid by an ally | the POV character risked nothing | `scene-craft` — whose scene is this |
| The win cost something, but only after it landed | the price was a receipt, not a stake | move the payment before the resolution |
| Every confrontation is won and costs nothing | the face-slap loop | `mtl-detox` |
| Three chapters running have the same cost | the ladder has stalled | `SKILL.md` §Tension maintenance across a serial |

## What it looks like when it lands

He gets the name. It costs him the only person at the yard who still spoke to him, and she does
not stop speaking to him dramatically — she just answers the next question with *"ask your
father"*. One concrete sentence, and the reader can price it.
