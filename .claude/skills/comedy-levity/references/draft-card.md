# comedy-levity — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` Phase A when the module is on. Two lines, and the honest answer for a
hard chapter is often none.

```
who      Berrow - dry, and it fires when he is frightened
where    early, over the inventory, before the summons arrives
```

## Decision 1 — whose

**Not yours to invent.** The `wit` axis in `bible/cast/_voices.md` already says who may be funny,
in which register, and under what pressure. At most two of the recurring cast have wit at all.
This card places a beat; it never hands a line to a character whose axis reads `none`, and it
never lets a scene make everybody funny because the scene had room. A cast where anyone can land
the joke has one voice in it (`voice-separation`).

The most revealing question about a funny character is **what they use it to avoid**. Today's beat
should be doing that.

## Decision 2 — where

Placement that works: early, before the pressure builds · after a heavy chapter, with the cost
still visible underneath · inside tension, as one person's coping mechanism, which raises tension
rather than releasing it.

Placement that fails: during the climax · immediately after a real loss · as the chapter hook,
unless the joke is also a threat.

## The test, applied after drafting the beat

> **Does the reader still believe the MC could lose?**

If the joke has made the danger feel unreal, cut it. Nothing else about the beat matters.

## While drafting

- **Undersell.** The narration never signals that a line was funny, and nobody laughs to cue the
  reader.
- **The last word is the funny word.** Sentence order does most of the work.
- **Do not explain.** The beat afterwards moves on.
- Nothing is funny at the expense of a group — humour is the commonest vector for the defect
  `bias-guard` owns, and that skill overrides this one.
