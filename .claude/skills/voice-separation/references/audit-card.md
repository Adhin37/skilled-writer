# voice-separation — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Pass 2**, every chapter. Written here rather than summarised there,
because this defect is distributional: it survives a clean tag-removal test, since the
fingerprints differ and the minds behind them do not.

`sw cast` has already settled the arithmetic — the straddle rule, the wit cap, the three-way
clash, turn and hand-habit collisions. What is left is the reading, and it is the whole job.

- [ ] **Transplant test** — the MC's most characteristic line does not fit any other character's
      mouth. If it does, they are the same person with two names
- [ ] At least one speaker differs from the MC on two or more axes: intel, articulacy, wit, heat,
      turn length, body
- [ ] No two speakers in a scene share intel + articulacy + wit
- [ ] Turn lengths held; nobody drifted toward the MC's
- [ ] Wit is confined to the characters who have it, at the pressure that triggers it. Nobody
      became funny because the scene had room
- [ ] Every action beat is that character's hand-habit or pressure move. Zero instances of
      *nodded, shrugged, sighed, raised an eyebrow, crossed their arms, let out a breath* used as
      identification — `sw lint` lists every occurrence with its line; deciding which are
      identification and which are incidental is the part it cannot do
- [ ] Non-POV minds shown through first moves; no second head entered
- [ ] Narration draws its metaphors from this POV's thought unit
- [ ] Any low-intel character is competent at their own work and right about something concrete.
      **Nobody exists in this chapter to be corrected**
- [ ] **Mirrors** — a clone, avatar or double sharing the original's voice is declared with
      `mirror:` and `convergence:`; what has diverged since `diverged_ch` shows on the page; the
      reader can tell which is speaking, or a character in the scene cannot either

**Voice deltas.** Any rung advance in `state/growth.md` is applied, was triggered and dramatised,
and is audible in dialogue — and no delta moved a character onto the MC's axes.

If a box fails, open `SKILL.md`: §3 for the three checks, §5 for the body channel, §6 for
anti-convergence, §7 for the mirror clause.
