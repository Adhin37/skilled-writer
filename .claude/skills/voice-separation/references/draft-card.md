# voice-separation — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` **Step 1**, every chapter with two or more speakers. Written here rather
than summarised there, because the default failure is **one mind wearing several names**, and it is
introduced at the moment the speakers are chosen — not at the moment the lines are written.

**Produce:** this chapter's speakers as matrix rows side by side, and which of them differs from the
MC on two or more axes.

The rows are in the read-set. Lay them out as a table before drafting; sameness is a property of a
*cast* and is invisible read one at a time.

| axis | values |
|---|---|
| **intel** | 1–5, the `mc-intel-meter` ladder |
| **artic** | 1 concrete nouns, abandoned sentences · 2 states the fact, never the reason · 3 ordinary competent · 4 builds an argument in one turn · 5 makes a wrong thing sound right |
| **wit** | none · dry · warm · clowning · cruel — **at most two of the recurring cast have any** |
| **heat** | flat · banked · quick · volatile |
| **turn** | default words per speaking turn — 4, 12, 25, 40. Mechanical. Hold it |
| **body** | one hand-habit + one pressure move |

**Intel and articulacy are independent, and the pairing is the character.** High intel / low artic
is right and cannot prove it. Low intel / high artic is wrong and convincing — the best non-villain
obstacle in fiction. Ordinary is a legitimate setting: most people are intel 2–3, artic 3, wit none,
and without that floor the exceptional ones do not read as exceptional.

## Three gates, before drafting

1. **MC-contrast.** At least one speaker differs from the MC on **two or more axes**. A chapter
   where everyone matches the MC's intel and articulacy has one character in it.
2. **Three-way clash.** No two speakers in this scene share intel **and** artic **and** wit. If they
   do, one has no reason to exist separately — re-cast or merge.
3. **Straddle.** Somebody in the recurring cast is above the MC's tier and somebody below.

`sw cast` settles all three as arithmetic over the table. It cannot see whether the drafted dialogue
obeys the table — that is Pass 2.

Any voice delta applied this chapter may **not** move a character onto the MC's axes
(`voice-separation` §6). A tier-C walk-on gets no row: one axis off default, and that is all.

Clones, avatars and doubles are the one exemption: `references/mirror-clause.md`. Bodies and
non-POV minds while drafting: `references/channels.md`.
