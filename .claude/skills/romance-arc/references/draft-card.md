# romance-arc — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` Phase A when the module is on. **None** is the answer in most chapters
of a `subplot` romance, and saying so is a decision rather than a skip.

**Produce two lines for the brief:**

```
beat     vulnerability - he tells her why he left, and she does not use it
costs    he is late to the meeting that was the point of the chapter
```

## Decision 1 — which beat, against the thread

The line is a thread in `state/threads.md` with its own `due` chapters. Read the row, then pick:
meet · friction · recognition · alliance · vulnerability · admission · obstacle · cost · reversal ·
resolution. The order and the timing live in `SKILL.md` §Structure; this card asks only whether
today's chapter moves one of them, and which.

**Resolve the stage, never the relationship.** In a serial, an answered question loses its
tension — an admission opens the question of what to do about it.

## Decision 2 — what it costs the other plot

A romance that costs nothing is a reward schedule. The beat should take something from the
chapter's other business: time, an ally's patience, a position, the thing they were supposed to be
doing. That cost is what makes the relationship matter rather than accumulate.

## While drafting

- **Intimacy escalates in trust, not in contact.** The strongest moves: telling the truth about
  something small · being seen doing something they are bad at · being trusted with a task ·
  noticing an absence · not explaining and being understood.
- **Attraction is specific and named.** A habit, a competence, an unexpected decency — physical
  description alone is interchangeable.
- **Let them be wrong about each other.** A half-right first impression is fifty chapters of
  material.
- The current obstacle is never a misunderstanding one conversation would fix. That is the weakest
  device in the form and readers name it immediately.
- Follow `content.rating`, and never write intimacy involving minors in any framing at any rating.
- `no-harem` and `bias-guard` bind whatever this card says.
