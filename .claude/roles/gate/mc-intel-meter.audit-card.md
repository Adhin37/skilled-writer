# mc-intel-meter — audit card

Opened by `revision-pass` **Passes 3 and 3b**. Intelligence and knowledge are two different things,
and the pass fails most often at the seam: a high tier waved through as a licence to know facts.
This card covers the intelligence half and the **reading-people** half; `competence-map`'s card
covers the knowledge half.

**Pass 3b is `social-perception`'s**, which owns the `eq` ladder, the emotional signatures and the
misread. It is audited here because a claim about what somebody feels is a knowledge claim and
needs a provenance the same way a fact does.

- [ ] Every MC decision passes the **trace test** — name the on-page fact behind it
- [ ] None of the seven floor rules violated
- [ ] Any MC failure uses missing information, opposed will, cost, or an established blind spot.
      Never the MC forgetting something they already know
- [ ] No narration asserting the MC is clever. Demonstrate; do not label
- [ ] **Nobody was made stupid to make the MC look smart**
- [ ] Tier-appropriate: no deduction above `mc.intel_tier`, no obtuseness below it

## Pass 3b — reading people (`social-perception`)

> **Did anyone state another character's interior state as a fact?**

*He had already decided to refuse her.* *She was frightened of her father.* Both report a second
head, which a limited viewpoint cannot enter; the prose has changed POV without announcing it. The
repair is one clause — hand the reader the evidence instead — and it reads better besides.

- [ ] Every conclusion about a person sits inside that character's `eq` tier
- [ ] The signal each reading rests on is on the page, in this chapter
- [ ] Nobody read everybody correctly, every time, without effort
- [ ] Feelings arrived as that person's **signature**, not the shared gesture set
- [ ] Any misread satisfies all four of `social-perception` §4's conditions
- [ ] Nobody was made socially blind so the MC could notice something

If a box fails, open `SKILL.md` — §The floor for the seven rules, §Three legal failures for the
repair — or `social-perception/SKILL.md` and `social-perception/references/reading-people.md`.

## What it looks like when it lands

She works out that the ledger was written to be found, because the ink is the same on all four
pages and she watched a clerk refill a pen in chapter 2. One planted fact, one inference, shown
once. She is still wrong about who left it, and that is the eq half.
