# World bible

Only facts that constrain the writing. If a detail would never change a sentence, leave it out.
Every entry must be **falsifiable on the page** — something a scene could contradict.

## Physical

| place | what it is | why it matters to the plot | sensory signature |
|---|---|---|---|

`sensory signature` = the two details that identify it instantly. Reuse them; that is how a
reader learns a place.

## Social order

- Who has power, and what is the power *made of* (land, violence, information, capital, magic)?
- How does an ordinary person get hurt by that order?
- What does the MC's position in it cost them daily?

## Factions

| faction | wants | method | fears | current posture toward MC |
|---|---|---|---|---|

No faction may be defined by an ethnicity, nationality, or bloodline that maps onto a real-world
group, and none may be uniformly evil. See `bias-guard`.
