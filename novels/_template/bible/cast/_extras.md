# Extras roster

Tier-C walk-ons: one to three scenes, then gone. **One line each. Never a profile.**

Maintained by `character-profile`. The job of this file is to keep a walk-on from being
interchangeable — not to understand them. Three strokes:

- **wants** — what they are trying to get through in the next five minutes. A five-minute goal.
- **tic** — one habit, physical or verbal, small enough to repeat if they come back.
- **carries** — one piece of the world: a price, a grievance, a rule they enforce, a rumour,
  something that changed at their work. This is the stroke that does the work.

Format:

```
<Name> — <what they do, where> — ch <appearances> — <status>
  wants: … | tic: … | carries: …
```

Promote to a real profile on a third appearance, or the moment they make a decision that changes
the plot — whichever comes first. Then move the line into `bible/cast/<slug>.md` and build the
profile *from what is already on the page*.

**Budget.** Past ~50 roster lines, split the roster into sections by location or faction and load
only the sections a chapter touches. A walk-on unreferenced for three arcs can be dropped; if they
reappear, they are a new sketch.
