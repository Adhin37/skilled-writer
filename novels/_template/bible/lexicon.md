## Terms of art

| term | capitalized? | italicized on first use? | plain-English meaning | anchor? | first appears |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**`anchor?`** — mark `yes` for any term that tells a new reader *what kind of story this is* or
*which world they are in*: the setting's name, the central power's name, the institution everyone
answers to, a canon proper noun in fan fiction. **Anchor terms are front-loaded, not saved.**
A term marked `anchor? yes` whose `first appears` is later than `opening.anchor_by_ch` is a
defect, not a slow burn — see `story-opening` §1. Deferring the word that names the genre does not
build mystery; it builds a reader who cannot tell what they are reading and leaves.

## House style

### The four channels

Ships filled in. Mirrors `novel.md` → `channels:`. Owned by `narrator-voice` §The four channels —
change it there and here together, or not at all.

| channel | mark | carries | budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speech | `"…"` | anything said aloud | 25–40% of the chapter |
| Direct thought | `'…'` | the verbatim sentence a character says to themselves and not aloud | **1–3 per chapter**, at decisions |
| Meta | `[…]` | system interfaces, status deltas, in-world documents, signs, narrative adjustments | as the story needs; never two in a row |
| Free indirect | *unmarked* | **the default carrier of interiority** | unlimited |

**Free indirect is the default and it is not marked.** *She wasn't looking for anything to take.
That was the part she'd have had trouble explaining.* — that is interiority, and it stays plain.
`'…'` is the exception, not the vehicle. A chapter where every interior beat wears quote marks has
turned its narrator into a thought bubble.

**The three collision rules.** These are not style preferences; without them the convention breaks.

1. **Apostrophes are not thought marks.** A thought opens at a word boundary and closes before
   punctuation or whitespace. `don't`, `she'd`, `the boys' room`, `'90s` are never thought.
2. **A `'…'` inside a `"…"` pair is an ordinary nested quotation**, as in any English prose:
   `"He actually said 'no comment' to my face."` A `'…'` outside any `"…"` pair is thought.
   Position disambiguates; there is no third mark.
3. **Nothing else is markup.** No bold, no headings, no lists, no links inside the prose body.
   `*italic*` has exactly one declared job — chosen below — and does not get a second.

### Meta block format

The shape a `[…]` block takes when it runs longer than a phrase — a status delta, a posted notice,
a letter, a system readout. Set it once. `litrpg-system` and `world-texture` both read this.

```
[ Echo-step — Rank 2 (was 1)
  Cost: 6 hours' recovery, down from 12 ]
```

Never opens a chapter. Never repeats an unchanged value. Never narrates the story.
