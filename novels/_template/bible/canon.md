> story stays consistent with it. It never stores, and the novel never reproduces, prose,
> dialogue, or lyrics from the source. All text written here and in the chapters is original.

## OOC budget

`ooc_budget` in `novel.md` sets how far characters may move from canon behaviour.

| budget | meaning |
|---|---|
| low | Behaviour must be canon-derivable. Change comes only from divergence-point consequences, shown on the page. |
| medium | One deliberate reinterpretation per major character, justified in this file. |
| high | Characters are re-founded; canon supplies premise and relationships only. Say so in the blurb. |
