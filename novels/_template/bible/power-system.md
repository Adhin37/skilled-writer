# Power system

Fantasy / scifi / progression only. Maintained by `power-system`. The reader must be able to
predict what is impossible; that is what makes a win feel earned instead of arbitrary.

## One-line pitch

## Source
Where the power comes from, and who controls the supply.

## The four hard rules

1. **Cost** — every use takes something measurable. State the unit.
2. **Limit** — the sentence beginning "this can never…". At least two.
3. **Learning curve** — how long a new capability takes, and what it costs to acquire.
4. **Counter** — how a weaker person defeats a stronger one. If there is no answer, the system
   is broken and the story has no tension after chapter 40.

## Progression ladder

Lives in `state/power.md` §2, not here — the ladder is needed in the read-set for every chapter,
and `bible/` is never loaded. Names for the tiers, if the world has them, go in `bible/lexicon.md`.

Keep tier *names* out of the prose as much as possible. Show tier through what a character can
and cannot do. Never open a chapter with a rank recital.

## The MC's specific kit

| ability | acquired ch | exact effect | cost | cooldown / limit | seen failing at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The last column is mandatory. An ability the reader has never seen fail is not yet a real ability.

## Escalation budget

Lives in `state/power.md` §4, where it is in the read-set and `sw curve` checks it. Every gain is
+1 tier and carries a source, a price paid first, a setup, and a new problem (`power-scaling` §3).

## Known unknowns
What even experts in-world do not understand. This is where late-story reveals come from.
