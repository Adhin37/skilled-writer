# World track

What the world does on its own clock, whether or not the MC is there. Maintained by
`timeline-engine`. The *log* of what actually happened lives in `state/timeline.md`.

Keep this coarse for original fiction — the antagonist's plan plus two or three standing clocks.
Keep it detailed for fan fiction, where it is the canon plot the MC is standing inside.

For fan fiction: record canon events as **your own one-line summaries of what happens and why**.
Never copy text from the source work into this file.

---

## 1. DRIVERS

Everyone and everything that makes events happen. Reaction profiles from `timeline-engine`.

| driver | profile | latency | fidelity | their actual top priority | posture toward MC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`profile`: `strategist` · `chaotic` · `institution` · `rival` · `indifferent`
`latency`: chapters between the MC acting and this driver answering
`fidelity`: how accurately they read what the MC actually did — `high` · `mixed` · `low`

**their actual top priority** is the governor's first rule on the page: for most drivers, most of
the time, it is not the MC. Write what it really is.

At least two profiles must be in play, and one should be `indifferent`.

## 2. WORLD TRACK

Events that fire without the MC. For fanfic, this is the canon sequence.

| id | event (your own words) | baseline ch | driver | what it needs to succeed | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`status`: `pending` · `moved` · `prevented` · `altered` · `fired` · `replaced`

**what it needs to succeed** is the most useful column. An MC does not prevent an event by being
strong; they prevent it by removing one of the things it needed.
