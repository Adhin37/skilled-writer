# Arc plan

An arc is `chapters.arc_length` chapters (default 25) with its own question, escalation and
answer. The novel is a stack of arcs; each ends with the MC changed and a larger problem visible.

## Arc template (copy per arc)

### Arc N — <title>  (ch A–B)

**Dramatic question.** One sentence, answerable yes/no by the arc's end.

**Situation shift.** Where the MC starts vs. ends — position, power, knowledge, relationships.
State all four; if one is unchanged, say so deliberately.

**Antagonistic force.** Who or what opposes, what *they* want, and why they are right from
their own seat. If you cannot argue their case in two sentences, the arc is not ready.

**Escalation ladder.** Five rungs, each raising the cost of failure.
1.
2.
3.
4.
5.

**Midpoint reversal.** The chapter where the MC's understanding of the problem is proven wrong.

**Cost.** What the MC permanently loses. Not "almost loses." Required.

**Character rungs advanced.** Which cast members move on their ladder, and on which chapter.

**Threads.** Opened here / paid here / deliberately left hanging into the next arc.

**Exit hook.** The image or line that makes a reader start the next arc immediately.
