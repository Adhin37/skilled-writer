# Form & appearance ledger

Maintained by `mc-design`. **Always in the read-set while any character has `form_locked: true`.**

> **The gate.** No sentence may describe a locked character's body, reach, voice, capability, or
> how strangers treat them except from their CURRENT FORM row below. Read the row before writing
> the sentence. Final-form details may never appear in an earlier stage.

Delete this file if no character changes form during the novel.

---

## 1. CURRENT FORM

The row that gets read. Update it the moment a transition fires.

| character | form | apparent age | build & bearing | the one detail | voice | since ch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`the one detail` — what a witness would report. Reuse it; it is how a reader tracks the body.

## 2. ABSOLUTE LIMITS — current stage

What the body cannot do right now. These are not obstacles to be forgotten in an action scene;
they may be worked around with cleverness and cost, never ignored.

| character | cannot physically | cannot socially | not believed about | needs help with |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Recognition anchor** — the thing that persists across every stage, by which someone who knew
them early could identify them later (a scar, a habit, a name, a way of holding still). Decide it
now; it is a payoff waiting to happen.

## 4. TRANSITION LOG

Every stage change, as it fires. Also gets a `bod>` line in that chapter's CCS block.

| ch | character | from → to | trigger | now enables | now costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

`now costs` is mandatory. A new form closes doors: the child who could go unnoticed cannot any
more; the ruined body that drew pity now draws suspicion.

## 5. MASKING LEDGER

For a mind older than its body. Tracks the gap between how the character behaves and what their
form leads people to expect.

| character | masking? | slips so far (ch) | who has noticed | what they concluded |
|---|---|---|---|---|

A slip is a scene, not an aside. Someone noticing is a plot event and belongs in `threads.md`.
