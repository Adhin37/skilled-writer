# Thread ledger

Every promise made to the reader, and its payoff status. Maintained by `plot-threads`.
A thread left `open` past its `due` chapter is a defect the next planning pass must address.

| id | thread | opened | type | tension | due | status | payoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**type**: `mystery` `promise` `debt` `threat` `relationship` `setup` `oath` `secret`
**tension**: `hot` (referenced within last 5 ch) · `warm` (last 15) · `cold` (dormant, still owed)
**status**: `open` · `escalated` · `paid` · `subverted` · `abandoned` (requires a note saying why)

---

## Dropped-thread quarantine

Threads gone cold for 25+ chapters. Each must be paid, deliberately subverted, or explicitly
retired on the page — never silently forgotten.
