# slice-of-life-texture — draft card

Opened by `write-chapter` Phase A when the module is on. One or two beats, decided now so they
ride inside a scene instead of stopping one.

**Produce one line for the brief:**

```
texture  the argument happens over the mending, and the chair is still broken
```

## Decision 1 — which channel

| channel | what it shows |
|---|---|
| Work | how someone earns; the part they are good at, the part they hate |
| Food | who cooks, what it cost, what it means when the good thing is made |
| Money | rent, debt, the price of the boots — specific numbers, held consistently |
| Repair | what a person's hands do at rest |
| Weather and season | affecting plans and moods, never opening a chapter |
| Sleep | where, how badly, next to whom |
| Small rituals | the order they do things in; what they always do before leaving |
| Idle talk | conversation that is not about the plot, between people who like each other |

Pull the facts from `bible/society.md` so the detail belongs to *this* world rather than being
generically cosy (`social-fabric` owns what things cost and who does which work).

## Decision 2 — where it rides

**Inside a scene that already has a goal.** The argument happens over the mending; the confession
happens while the stew burns. A chapter that is only texture is a chapter readers skip, and in a
serial that habit spreads to the next one.

Budget: one or two beats. A whole downtime chapter earns its place about once per arc — after a
heavy loss, or immediately before a known danger, where it works as dread.

## The test

> **Could the plot take this away later?**

Every domestic detail established is something the story can threaten. That is the whole
mechanism: texture converts abstract stakes into concrete ones, and a reader who watched someone
repair the roof will feel it burn. A detail nothing could ever take is decoration.

**Specificity or nothing.** *They ate dinner* is not texture. *She ate standing up because the
chair was still broken* is character, economics and mood in nine words.

`world-texture` still governs how much description is allowed to reach the page at all; its budget
binds here too.
